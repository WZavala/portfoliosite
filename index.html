Background

From the classroom to enterprise learning architecture.

My approach to instructional design is shaped by seeing learning from every angle: as a high school mathematics teacher, a Director of Technology leading campus-wide digital transformation, a freelance instructional designer for adult learners, and now as part of the learning technology team at my two-time alma mater, the University of Central Florida.

Over a 15-year career in education and technology, I’ve learned that the best curriculum or software rollout only succeeds when it respects the end user’s time and cognitive load. Whether I’m building a SCORM-compliant interactive simulation, redesigning a hybrid course, or guiding stakeholders through a system transition with Prosci ADKAR, my focus is clarity, usability, and measurable impact.
